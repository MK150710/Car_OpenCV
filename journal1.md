# My first OpenCV project

Heyy.... So ive always wanted to work with openCV and python, and as a kid growing up, me and my brother used to play This lil game called poly track..
<br>
Poly track is basically a racing game in which you try to beat your previous time, it soulds lousy, but can get addictive real fasttt, i can tell from firsthand experiencee haha

## Main Goal of the project

The main goal is to give polyTrack a new side... Playing with your hands as the steering.. now, as i dont have an ai model to host locally, this will not be 100% accurate.
<br>
But thats the fun of itt.. The rugged play, new techniques and all the fun stuff.. Still, imma try to make htis as likeable as possible

## What ive done till now

First of all, to clarify, I initially wanted this to be a way to play smash karts, but its not really easy to play a multiplayer game with a huge disadvantage, i kept dying all the time...
<br>
So ive finally decided to scrap that and move to Poly Track. I mean its still a great game

Moving on, till now i have:

- Quite precisely recognised hands and created a way to recognise the button presses(thumb presses) and right-left movement of hands, that part is accurate
- I have added a timer so that it doesnt click the reset button multiple times and it works, ive tested it
